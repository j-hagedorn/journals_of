**+**
  
Riven, I arrive.

**+**
  
Hand to my chest,  
fumbling to untie, unclasp,  
this nest of wasps.

**+**
  
Brushing a hand to earth,  
I sow  
my hunger in its belly.  
  
**+**
  
Even the gawdy mind,  
its phosphorescent 
  
pomace, will rest  
in that dark gut.

**+**

At the base of that abyss,  
the self, is not a point  

but a crossing - no simpler shape suffices.  

**+**
  
Crude, irrefutable mark,  
the stick-person I  
retrace into my caverns.  

**+**

A talisman against distraction:  
“one-pointedness” in Zen.  
 
Confluence of interminable routes,  
“Here, I AM.”  

**+**
  
My attention betrays itself  
reducible to my hungers.  
  
I bless my arms: lie  
still at my sides.  

**+**
  
We begin the incision  
in the forehead  
as with any little god.  

**+**

  *"On the crown of the head*  
  *... a morsel of uncut stone*  
   
  *... by one touch to maintain*  
  *... connexion with the place*  
  *... which it was hewn."*[^1]  
  
**+**

The ground pulls down the plum  
snapped from its reaches  

which thumps and rolls to a periphery,  
where flesh molds and becomes  

a new, brief center.  

**+**
  
I draw the line  
from star to stone,  

then unfold the horizon  
by walking it.  

**+**
  
*"Who will lead me to that... dimmer region*  
*where Thought weds Fact...*  

*the... mathematician and... the molecules*  
*seen in their true relation?*"[^2]  

**+**
  
Speaking, I affix a string  
from signified to signifier,  

then pluck the chord,  
back and forth.  

**+**
  
Songs mingle, crescendo, crash: do  
what time and music do.  

Crest and spread.  

**+**
  
Draw the three fingers  
from the fore-skull to the belly.  

Draw three raindrops  
from storm into soil.  

**+**
  
As ashes rewind, flame  
recalls the tree:  
  
as a tree re-dreams  
the gnarled gauntlet of its birth,  
  
you pry the sky-seed  
open, wary, side to side.  

**+**
  
The cross that I trace on myself  
is the thief's.  

**+**
  
As above, so below.  
As with you, so with me.  
  
Under all eloquence, elegance:  
profusion blooming from resemblance.  

**+**

Me but not mine.  

I am in my body and in the pathways outside of my body.  

**+**

Hope is the loss  
I bury in you, and never see.  

Thank you for being  
my child, that blest  
kernel of horizon in my chest:  
  
A way  
into here.  



[^1]: Walter Pater, *The Renaissance: Studies in Art and Poetry* (1837)

[^2]: James Clerk Maxwell, *Address to the Mathematical and Physical Sections of the British Association*. From the _British Association Report_, Vol. XL. Liverpool, _September_ 15, 1870.
